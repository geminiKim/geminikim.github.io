A company can provide work and unfamiliar experiences. It cannot convert those experiences into a developer's growth on their behalf. Growth happens when the developer gathers experience and makes it their own.

Choosing a team that offers useful experience is sensible. The distinction is between seeking good material for growth and expecting the organization to perform the learning for you.

## Experience does not become growth automatically

Work is one source of experience. Books, courses, side projects, and communities are others. Merely doing the work, watching the course, touching the project, or attending the community does not automatically produce growth. Accumulated experience becomes growth only when the developer absorbs it and makes it their own.

## The developer remains responsible

It is reasonable to ask whether a company offers unfamiliar work and useful experience. A developer can leave a team that offers no path for the experience they seek and choose a different environment.

The employer pays for work and may supply useful experience, but the developer is responsible for turning that experience into growth. A person may choose a different team for better experience; they still cannot demand that the company do the growing for them.
